Railway Ticket Reservation System

Search trains, check seat availability, book tickets, cancel reservations and manage train records, all from one application.

What would you like to do?

  • Passengers Search Trains Find trains by route or train number and check seat availability.
  • Passengers Book a Ticket Enter passenger details and receive a PNR, seat number and fare.
  • Passengers Cancel a Ticket Cancel with your PNR and see the refund amount.
  • Staff Admin Login Sign in to verify administrator access through the C++ backend.

Cancel a Ticket

Enter your PNR to retrieve your booking details. Review the details before confirming cancellation. After a successful cancellation, the seat is released and the refund amount is displayed. The refund is 90% of the original stored fare.
